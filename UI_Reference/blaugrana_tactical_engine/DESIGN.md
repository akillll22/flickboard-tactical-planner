## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges elite European football heritage with high-precision analytical intelligence. It communicates authority, tactical rigor, and cutting-edge telemetry tailored for top-tier coaching staff, performance analysts, and tactical visionaries. The aesthetic avoids casual consumer gamification, favoring the sophisticated, focused aura of an elite dugout terminal and professional sports analytics platform.

The design movement combines **Deep Analytical Dark Mode** with **Subtle Tactical Glassmorphism**:
- Deep obsidian and charcoal slate structural layers mimic illuminated technical control rooms.
- Signature Blaugrana hues provide disciplined identity anchors rather than overwhelming surface fills.
- High-contrast tactical neon accents (electric pitch green, gold telemetry indicators, warning vermilion) deliver immediate visual clarity for pitch movements, heat maps, and live match data.
- UI elements feature crisp micro-borders, semi-translucent backdrop blurs, and technical data density balanced by purposeful spacing.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for deep-contrast tactical visualization under low-light coaching conditions:

- **Primary (`#004d98` - Royal Blaugrana Blue):** Anchors primary control systems, active tactical roles, positional lines, and primary callouts.
- **Secondary (`#a50044` - Deep Blaugrana Crimson):** Represents opponent heat patterns, high-intensity pressing triggers, attacking zones, and critical tactical alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#edbb00` - Senyera Gold):** Reserved for elite player status, key tactical breakthroughs, captain telemetry, and trophy/championship metrics.
- **Tactical Pitch Green (`#00e676` / `#10b981`):** Functional accent for pitch gridlines, ball trajectories, passing success rates, and optimal stamina indicators.
- **Dark Neutral Stack:**
  - Base Canvas: `#0a0f18` (Obsidian Pitch Ground)
  - Surface Tier 1: `#111827` (Card & Dock Foundation)
  - Surface Tier 2: `#1e293b` (Elevated Panels & Flyouts)
  - Border Contrast: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`
- **Data Status Colors:**
  - Success/Passing Accuracy: `#00e676`
  - Fatigue / Caution: `#f59e0b`
  - High Injury Risk / Tactical Breaches: `#ef4444`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy distinguishes operational data from editorial narrative:

- **Space Grotesk (Headlines):** Delivers a technical, sharp, and structured presence for match phases, scorelines, tactical board headers, and team formations (e.g., `4-3-3 POSSESSION ATTACK`).
- **Inter (Body):** Ensures optimal scannability across dense coaching notes, match reports, substitution strategies, and player bio details.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry & Labels):** Applied strictly to real-time tactical statistics, pitch coordinates (`X: 42.4, Y: 88.1`), player jersey numbers, xG counters, and telemetry tags. All numbers are rendered with tabular figures to avoid layout jitter during live calculations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a high-efficiency **Adaptive Multi-Panel Canvas**:

- **Desktop & Tactical Tablets (Landscape):** A 12-column layout dividing the viewport into three core zones: Left Tactical Sidebar (2 cols), Center 2D/3D Interactive Pitch Simulator (7 cols), and Right Live Telemetry & Substitutions Inspector (3 cols). Gutters maintain a tight `1rem` rhythm to conserve visual real estate for the pitch board.
- **Mobile (Portrait):** Stacks the pitch visualization pinned to the top 45vh with touch-draggable player nodes, while phase instructions and player stats reside in a swipeable bottom sheet. Margins compress to `1rem` to maximize touch precision on smaller screens.
- **Component Spacing:** Component internal padding uses strict multiples of `0.25rem` (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) to establish dense, instrument-grade control clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on calibrated luminescence and surface attenuation rather than heavy muddy dropshadows:

1. **Pitch Base Canvas (Level 0):** Flat obsidian `#0a0f18` overlaid with an ultra-subtle SVG tactical pitch grid (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`).
2. **Glass Container Panels (Level 1):** Background `#111827` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an inner top border highlight of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Floating Floating Controls & Flyouts (Level 2):** Background `#1e293b` with a dual-layer shadow:
   - Ambient: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`
   - Rim: `0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
4. **Active Tactical Node Glow (Level 3 - Interactive):** Selected player markers and live passing lanes project localized neon halos:
   - Selected Player (Home): `0 0 16px rgba(0, 77, 152, 0.6)` with a secondary ring of `#edbb00`.
   - Passing Vector: `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 6px #00e676)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft / Precision Engineering** shape language (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Micro Tokens & Metric Badges:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius for compact telemetry indicators, positional tags (e.g., `PIV`, `EXT`, `INT`), and data inputs.
- **Cards, Pitch Overlays, and Drawers:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius, projecting an agile, calibrated software aesthetic reminiscent of professional avionics or telemetry hardware.
- **Player Nodes:** Circular (`50%`), surrounded by multi-stroke tactical status halos indicating stamina, yellow cards, and active ball possession.
- **Pills:** Used strictly for live match state markers (e.g., `● LIVE 64:12`) and tactical preset toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Tactical Action:** Deep Blaugrana Blue (`#004d98`) background with subtle inner bevel, high-contrast crisp white typography, and a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Hover triggers a glowing cyan-blue perimeter bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(0, 77, 152, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary / Defensive Trigger:** Deep Blaugrana Crimson (`#a50044`), reserved for press traps, offside line triggers, or destructive plan wipes.
- **Ghost / Tool Bar Icon Buttons:** Translucent slate background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), yielding to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` on active hover.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- **Player Position Badges:** Compact monospace badges with `0.25rem` radius. Blue-tinted for defensive block, gold-tinted for playmakers, crimson-tinted for dynamic wingers/forwards.
- **Metric Pill:** Features a mini tabular number indicator alongside a tactical neon green dot indicating positive performance metrics (e.g., `+0.34 xG`).

### Cards & Tactical Modules
- **Analytical Cards:** Built with `#111827` backing, 1px perimeter stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`), and subtle top-left Blaugrana corner gradient accent strips.
- **Player Roster Card:** Displays jersey number in `JetBrains Mono`, player face cutout, current energy bar, and tactical role icon.

### Form Inputs & Adjusters
- **Tactical Steppers & Sliders:** Sleek horizontal line controls with glowing Blaugrana or Neon Green slider thumbs to dynamically adjust team metrics like Defensive Line Height, Compactness, and Width.
- **Inputs:** Dark `#0a0f18` wells with inset stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, transitioning to `#004d98` glowing outline upon focus.

### Interactive Tactical Pitch Nodes (App-Specific)
- **Player Tokens:** Two concentric rings; outer ring reflects player stamina depletion in neon green, inner disc carries player number in `JetBrains Mono` over team colors (Blaugrana stripes or solid gold for captain).
- **Vector Connectors:** Dashed interactive bezier curves with animated glowing dash arrays depicting planned movement paths, counter-press trajectories, and through-ball passing channels.
